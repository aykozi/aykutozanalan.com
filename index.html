I began my career as a deputy inspector at the Turkish Grain Board (TMO), a state economic enterprise of 35 directorates and 190 units, carrying out financial, operational and compliance audits and later leading audit teams as Head Inspector.

Since 2023 I have been Internal Audit Manager at Limak Group, leading internal audit across 133 companies in Turkey, the Middle East and Europe, in construction, energy, cement, airport operations, food and tourism.

I hold a PhD in Political Science and Public Administration from Middle East Technical University, and I write about governance, internal control and fraud prevention.
